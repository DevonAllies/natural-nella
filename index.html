Our story

Relax. Refresh. Recharge.

Founded in 2020 at the height of the pandemic, Naturally Nella (Pty) Ltd began as a mobile hair and beauty service dedicated to specialized hair and body treatments. This hands-on experience inspired us to create our own line of handmade bath and body products, allowing us to bring the spa experience directly to your home.

Today, we specialize in high-quality, handmade bath and body products as well as room and home fragrances. We take pride in crafting items that our customers love—people who appreciate the use of natural ingredients and enjoy filling their homes with beautiful aromas.

Looking to the Future

Our vision is to expand our offering by reintroducing pamper parties, creating a space where clients can enjoy a hands-on experience while having our handmade products available for purchase. We look forward to growing our community and continuing to provide natural, handmade self-care solutions.
